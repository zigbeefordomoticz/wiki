# Update the ZiGate firmware

ZiGate coordinators are no longer supported by the plugin. The old procedure has been archived: [Update the ZiGate firmware (archived)](Archives/HowTo_Update-ZiGate-firmware.md).

To update a ZiGate, get the firmware and the flashing instructions from the ZiGate releases: [ZiGate V1](https://github.com/fairecasoimeme/ZiGate/releases), [ZiGate+ V2](https://github.com/fairecasoimeme/ZiGateV2/releases), and [zigate.fr (in French)](https://zigate.fr/documentation/mise-a-jour-de-la-zigate).

We advise moving to a Texas Instruments, Silicon Labs or deCONZ coordinator: see [Compatible coordinators](Coordinators.md) and [Replace or migrate the coordinator](HowTo_Replace-coordinator.md).
